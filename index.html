Combat log

    Operation:

    JUGGERNAUT

    A self-aware siege tank the size of a city block is rolling toward your command post. It does not stop.

    Mode
    Juggernaut

    How to play

    Goal. The Juggernaut wins by destroying the Command Post (any hit kills it, or it can simply roll over it). A total victory means it also escapes back off the east edge. The defense wins by immobilizing the Juggernaut before it gets there.

    Turn sequence

    1. Juggernaut moves — up to 3 hexes (fewer as treads are lost). Entering a hex with armor rams it: the unit is destroyed, the Juggernaut loses 1 tread (2 for a Heavy Tank). Running over infantry crushes one squad.
    2. Juggernaut fires — each weapon once. Weapons can combine on one target. Missiles are one-shot. AP guns only hit infantry and the CP.
    3. Defense moves, then fires — units can combine fire on one Juggernaut system. Shots at treads are always 1:1; a hit removes treads equal to that unit's attack.
    4. Hovertanks move again (3 MP) — shoot and scoot.

    Combat

    Compare attack to defense, round down to odds, roll a die. X destroys, D disables (unit skips its next turn; a second D destroys it; infantry lose a squad instead). Juggernaut systems ignore D. Worse than 1:2 can't hurt; 6:1 or better is automatic.

    Units

    Terrain: craters are impassable; rubble costs armor 2 MP. Hover any unit to see its range. Right-click or Esc clears a selection; Enter ends the phase.

    A tribute to the classic microarmor board and computer wargames of the late '70s and '80s.
